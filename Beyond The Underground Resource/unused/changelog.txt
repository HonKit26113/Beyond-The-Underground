====================
New Cave Type: Ice Caves!
 - These caves generate exclusively under frozen biomes
 - Ice can be found everywhere, including dripping icicles and ice crystals
 - Lost Explorers have settled down in these caves because they couldn't find a way out

New Mobs:
 - Penguin
 - Lost Explorer

New Blocks:
 - Pebbles (Normal, Cobblestone, Frosted)
 - Frosted Stone
 - Crystallized Ice
 - Ice Crystal
 - Dripping Icicle
 - Packed Ice Bricks (Normal, Cracked, Slab)
 - Chiseled Packed Ice
 - Deepslate Glow Mycelium
 - Slimy Deepslate
 - Suitcase

New Items:
 - Ice Shard
 - Potion of Freezing (Slowness VII, Mining Fatigue III, 0:20)
 - Potion of Freezing (Slowness VII, Mining Fatigue III, 0:40)

Other Changes:
 - Added new custom loading messages!
 - Deepslate Glow Mycelium and Slimy Deepslate will now generate instead of their Stone counterparts when they are replacing Deepslate.
 - Patches of Calcite will now generate in Limestone Caves.
 - Limestone Bricks, Polished Limestone and Ice Bricks can now be crafted using a Stonecutter.
 - It's now harder to get out of Quicksand.
 - Some blocks will now have their textures rotated randomly.
 - You now have to interact with the top/bottom (depending on the block) of Radiant Vines, Dripping Slime, Sandy Roots and Dripping Icicles to place another one, to match Vanilla dripstone behavior.
 - The subpack system has been changed, once again. You now have to choose what Minecraft version you're on so the add-on can function properly.
 - Custom blocks in this add-on will now be in the creative menu in Minecraft 1.17.20 and later versions!
 - Removed temporary items to place custom blocks if the 1.17.20 or 1.17.30 subpack is selected.
 - New pack icon!
 - New background panorama!
 - This add-on now requires Minecraft 1.17.10 or above to function.

Fixed Bugs:
 - Luminite armor now renders correctly. (thanks SoriPop!)
 - Vanilla armor and armor from other add-ons now render correctly. (thanks SoriPop!)
 - Items added to the creative inventory for placing blocks no longer appear massive when held. (thanks Laiffoo!)
 - Blocks now have the correct amount of blast resistance.
 - Scorpions and Slimy Zombies can now despawn, reducing lag.
 - The Shroom Elf's legs will no longer detach from its body when looking in certain angles.
 - Scorpions will no longer "lift up" when looking in certain angles.
 - Scorpions will no longer use the wrong model on some devices.
 - The time needed to break certain blocks has been changed.
 - The Quicksand icon is no longer slightly smaller than other blocks.
 - The correct item now drops when breaking Dripping Slime and Sandy Roots.
 - 1 Sand Layer will now be used when stacking them on top of another one.
 - The texture of triple-stacked Sand Layers now appear normal.
 - The hitbox of Slime Layers is now 1-px tall to match their appearance.
 - Luminous Mushroom Stems can once again be rotated.
 - Durability will now be used when breaking blocks using Luminite tools.
 - Luminite Shovels can now break certain blocks quicker.
 - Fixed a typo in the code of Luminite Leggings.
 - Custom items added in this add-on are now put into the correct categories and item groups.
 - "Invalid lang file format" warnings will no longer appear.
