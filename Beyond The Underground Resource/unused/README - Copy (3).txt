Changelog
Version 1.5
Released August 17, 2023

New Blocks:
 - Slimy Pebbles
 - Slimy Deepslate Pebbles
 - Crooked Door

New Items:
 - Slingshot
 - Sandstone Stick
 - Sandy Skelly Skull
 - Wand of the Sands
 - Frost Essence
 - Frozen Core
 - Bane of Frost

New Mob:
 - Sandy Skelly
 - Frost Spirit

New Structure:
 - Ice Dungeon

Changes:
 + General Changes:
	- Added custom background music to all cave types! Massive thanks to Leonardo!
	  	Luminous Caverns: Leonardo - Gleaming in the Dark
	  	Slimy Sewers: Leonardo - Lost in Ooze
	  	Limestone Caves: Leonardo - Undead Winds
	  	Ice Caves: Leonardo - Below Zero
	  	Crooked Caverns: Leonardo - Deeper Depths
	- New panorama featuring the updated Slimy Sewers!
	- New loading screen tips!
	- Potions no longer have enchantment glints.
	- Slightly changed the model of pebbles.
	- This add-on now requires Minecraft 1.20.10 or above to function.
+ Luminous Caverns:
	- Changed the texture of the Block of Luminite.
	- Luminite gear can now be destroyed by explosions as the minecraft:explodable property was removed by Mojang Studios.
	- Reduced the amount of durability of all Luminite gear:
	  	Luminite Helmet: 490 --> 381 (-109)
	  	Luminite Chestplate: 711 --> 554 (-157)
	  	Luminite Leggings: 667 --> 520 (-147)
	  	Luminite Boots: 578 --> 450 (-217)
	  	All Luminite tools: 2571 --> 1795 (-776)
	- All Luminite tools are now affected by the Efficiency enchantment.
+ Slimy Sewers:
	- Slimy Zombies now shoot Hardened Slimeballs using Slingshots!
	- Slingshots can be used to shoot Hardened Slimeballs. If you pull back the slingshot longer, the Hardened Slimeball shot deals more damage!
	- Reduced the amount of damage Hardened Slimeballs deal (7 --> 5).
	- When placing torches on Slime Layers, the layer will be replaced by the torch instead of the torch floating on top of it.
	- Trail particles now appear when walking on top of Slimy Stone and Slimy Deepslate.
	- Mud patches now generate in the Slimy Sewers. 
	- Tall Grass & Large Ferns now generate in the Slimy Sewers.
	- Slimy Pebbles & Slimy Deepslate Pebbles now generate in the Slimy Sewers.
	- Slime Dungeons now generate less frequently.
	- Slimy Blossoms now give off more light (4 --> 6).
	- Slimy Stone no longer generates below y=0.
	- Changed the friction value of some Slimy blocks for consistency.
	- Slime Daggers now have enchantment glints.
	- Witches now spawn less frequently.
+ Limestone Caves:
	- Thrown Sand Lumps now deal more damage (1 --> 4).
	- Limestone Dungeons now generate less frequently and only up to y=40.
	- Limestone Ruins now generate more frequently and up to y=50.
	- Quicksand now generate less commonly in Limestone Caves.
	- Coarse Dirt, Dead Bushes and Tall Grass now generate in the Limestone Caves.
	- Cobbled Limestone now generate less commonly.
	- When placing torches on Sand Layers, the layer will be replaced by the torch instead of the torch floating on top of it.
	- Reduced the amount of diamonds found in Limestone Dungeon chests.
	- Increased the amount of valuables in Limestone Dungeon chests.
+ Ice Caves:
	- Potions of Freezing are now brewed with Frost Essence instead of Ice Crystals.
	- Ice Dungeons now generate between y=-40 and y=40.
	- Lost Explorers now trade 2 Honey Bottles for 2 Diamonds.
	- Lost Explorers now trade 1 Diamond for 1 Frost Essence.
	- Removed the Lost Explorers' trade of flowers for Ice Crystals.
	- Other than the custom-made song by Leonardo, 2 other songs by C418 also play in the Ice Caves:
		C418 - Kyoto
		C418 - Flake
+ Crooked Caverns:
	- Upside down mini Crooked Forests now generate at the ceiling of Nether Wastes!
	- Crooked Caverns now generate much more frequently.
	- Crooked Caverns are now more cavernous!
	- Glowing Obsidian now generate less commonly at lower levels of the cave.
	- Gold Lava Tanks (all variants) now take 0.5 seconds longer to destroy.
	- Diamond Lava Tanks (all variants) are now more blast resistant. (2 --> 3)
	- Wisps now have larger hitboxes, making them easier to kill.
	- The Wither effect from Wisps' thrown souls now lasts a shorter period of time.

Fixed Bugs:
 - Updated the add-on to be compatible for Minecraft 1.20.10 & 1.20.20+.
 - Glow Mycelium no longer generates below y=0.
 - Entities no longer gain a speed boost when walking on Quicksand, Slimy Stone and Slimy Deepslate blocks.
 - Entities now move slower on Slimy Deepslate Slabs.
 - All Slimy Deepslate blocks now take the same amount of time to destroy.
 - Fixed a misplaced pixel in the texture of Slime Lanterns.
 - The Slime Layer & Sand Layer crafting recipes now give the correct items.
 - Fixed an issue causing Limestone Caves to not generate.
 - Scorpions no longer spawn in peaceful difficulty.
 - Sandstone patches no longer generate below y=0 in Deserts.
 - Blue fog now also appears in caves in Frozen Ocean biomes.
 - Crooked Fences and Soul Brick Fences now have correct collision boxes.
 - Nether cave carvers once again generate in versions 1.20.20 and above.
 - Fixed an issue that could cause water to generate in the Nether.
