====================
New Blocks:
 - Gloomy Mushroom
 - Gloomy Mushroom Block
 - Mysterious Fungus
 - Slimy Stone Bricks (Normal, Cracked, Slab)
 - Slimy Deepslate Bricks (Normal, Cracked, Slab)
 - Slimy Deepslate Tiles (Normal, Cracked, Slab)
 - Slime Lantern
 - Sticky Spikes
 - Deepslate Pebbles
 - Cobbled Deepslate Pebbles
 - Limestone Pebbles
 - Cobbled Limestone Pebbles
 - Limestone Slab
 - Frosted Stone Slab

New Items:
 - Mysterious Stew
 - Slime Dagger
 - Lump of Sand

New Mob:
 - Luminous Mooshroom

Changes:
 - Changed textures of Luminite Ore and Deepslate Luminite Ore.
 - Changed textures of Glow Mycelium, Glow Mycelium, Luminous Mushrooms, Luminous Mushroom Blocks, and Radiant Vines.
 - Luminous Mushrooms now give off slightly more light. (7.5 > 8.25)
 - Changed the crafting recipe of Luminous Mushroom Stew.
 - Luminous Mushroom Stems can no longer be rotated, to match vanilla.
 - There is now fog in Luminous Caverns, to match the other cave types.
 - Changed the texture of Hardened Slimeballs to better match vanilla.
 - Witches now spawn in the Slimy Sewers.
 - Slimy Blossoms and Slimy Sprouts can now be placed on Slime Blocks.
 - Slimeballs can no longer be crafted back into Slimeball Parts.
 - Slime Layers have been changed to work like Snow and Sand Layers, and now drops the correct amount of Slimeball Parts accordingly.
 - Ruins can now be found in Limestone Caves!
 - Limestone Pebbles and Cobbled Limestone Pebbles now generate in Limestone Caves.
 - Sand Layers now generate in Limestone Caves.
 - The probabilities of loot in Limestone Dungeon chests have been changed.
	 - Diamonds, Golden Apples, and Enchanted Apples now generate more frequently.
 - Limestone Dungeons now always generate with a Sandstone ceiling.
 - Sand Layers now drop 1-2 Lumps of Sand when broken. These can be crafted back into blocks of sand.
 - Suitcases can now be crafted, but those cannot be used to summon a Lost Explorer.
 - Penguins can now be lured using Raw Cod and Raw Salmon.
 - Luminite Ore now generates in smaller veins.
 - Luminite Ore now drops 1-2 Luminite Shards when broken.
 - Luminite now has a shapeless recipe and requires only 1 Diamond and 6 Luminite Shards to craft.
 - The maximum durability of all Luminite tools and armor have been increased:
	 - Luminite Helmet: 445 > 490 (+45)
	 - Luminite Chestplate: 646 > 711 (+65)
	 - Luminite Leggings: 605 > 667 (+61)
	 - Luminite Boots: 525 > 578 (+53)
	 - All Luminite Tools: 2337 > 2571 (+234)
 - Reorganized custom items from this add-on in the creative inventory.
 - New resource pack icon to match textures changes!
 - New custom loading messages!
 - New background panorama!
 - New Minecraft-style Beyond The Underground title, thanks to MCSASA!

Fixed Bugs:
 - Shroom Elves can now spawn on Deepslate Glow Mycelium.
 - The Slimy Blossom no longer has model errors.
 - The Slimy Blossom will now drop itself when broken.
 - Dripping Slime now works correctly and no longer throws errors
 - Ores now generate correctly in Limestone Caves.
 - Scorpions no longer spawn at negative Y levels.
 - Scorpions now only drop 1 Raw Scorpion item on death. The Looting enchantment no longer increases the amount you get.
 - Sandstone and Quicksand no longer generate at negative Y levels in Deserts.
 - Frosted Deepslate Pebbles can now be crafted using a Stonecutter.
